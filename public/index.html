FAQ

Questions

What is Helm?

Helm is an AI agent that manages a portfolio of tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. You choose a risk level, and it builds and rebalances the portfolio for you.

Does Helm hold my money?

No. Your stocks sit in a vault that you own. The agent is allowed to rebalance inside it, but only you can withdraw.

When does it launch?

The agent is in development. Waitlist members get access in waves, starting with the first wave and the people with the most referrals.

Do I need $HELM to use it?

Holders get in first and unlock extra tools. Full details come before launch on @helmdotlol.

Is this financial advice?

No. Helm is software that follows the rules you set. Stocks and tokens can lose value, so only put in what you can afford to lose.
